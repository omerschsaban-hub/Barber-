# 20× acceptance criteria

The implementation is considered complete only when the existing UI/MCP surfaces can drive one evidence chain without inventing evidence:

1. A STEP revision has a stable hash and kernel-derived topology.
2. Features are deterministic or explicitly marked as CV-derived.
3. DFM violations identify the affected feature/rule and deterministic fix provenance.
4. Revalidation proves whether the fix actually cleared the violation.
5. Physics simulations carry declared parameters, seed, solver/provenance and uncertainty.
6. Physical tests specify what must be measured and why.
7. CV measurements refuse when scale/alignment is not defensible.
8. Physical observations are immutable evidence inputs.
9. Residuals are calculated from prediction and observation, never generated by the model.
10. Residual ML/system-identification models expose held-out validation before calibration claims.
11. Calibration records the model version and evidence provenance.
12. Next-test selection is based on information value/cost/risk and remains human-executable.
13. CAD/physics/CV/physical/ML disagreements are explicit.
14. LLM output is traceable to evidence and cannot promote itself to engineering truth.
15. Release remains blocked until required physical evidence, residual evidence, validation and human approval are present.

This is the definition of the 20× loop: improve the evidence closure, not the number of buttons.
